Water Assets - separated PNG crops

This archive contains individual transparent PNG crops extracted from the generated water asset sheet.
The preview_contact_sheet.png shows the exported objects.

Contents include water tiles/pieces, shore sections, waterfalls, ponds, rocks, reeds, lily pads,
water plants, ripples, splashes, currents, whirlpool effects, dock, boat, and bridge parts where
the artwork could be isolated as a separate connected object.

Important:
- These are individual visual crops, not a calibrated Godot TileSet.
- Some connected tile groups may remain together if they touch in the source image.
- Dimensions vary; they are not all 32x32.
- Review and adjust/slice in a pixel editor before importing into production.
